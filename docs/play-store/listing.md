## Full description (4,000 characters max)

FitMetric is a calorie counter and macro tracker that adapts to you. It builds your daily calories, protein, carbs and fat from your stats and goal, then learns your real calorie burn from what you eat and how your weight moves. No account, no ads, and your food log stays on your phone.

CALORIES THAT ADAPT TO YOU
• Weekly check-in: measures your real daily burn (TDEE) from your food log and weight trend, then suggests a new calorie target
• Choose how fast to lose or gain weight, and your targets follow
• This week: your calorie balance for the week, right on Today

SEE WHAT DINNER DOES TO YOUR WEIGHT
• Next-day weight shows which dinners raise or lower the next morning's weigh-in
• See the biggest jumps, the dinners that help, and how long the effect lasts
• A heads-up while you log a dinner that usually bumps the scale

KNOW WHEN YOU'LL REACH YOUR GOAL
• Goal forecast: a likely date for your target weight, from your real trend
• What if: try a different daily target and see the new date
• Pick a date and see the calories you'd need to get there
• Add your own milestones, like a wedding or a holiday

NOT SURE WHAT TO EAT? SEE WHAT FITS
• What fits? suggests foods you already eat, at your usual amounts, that fit the calories and macros you have left
• Filter by breakfast, lunch, dinner or snack

LOG FOOD IN SECONDS
• Barcode scanner for packaged foods
• Online food search, plus a built-in library of 290 common foods
• Recent foods remember the portion you had last time
• Favorites, quick add, custom foods and recipes
• Copy a meal or a whole day, repeat meals, and plan tomorrow
• Food history: every time you ate a food, and how much

HOME SCREEN WIDGETS
• Today, Water, Weight, Streak and Quick log widgets
• Add water in one tap without opening the app, with undo
• Choose the details, buttons and colours each widget shows

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Weight history with a 3, 5, 7 or 10-day moving average
• Body measurements: waist, chest, hips, arms, thighs and more
• Progress photos with a before & after slider and photo reminders
• Share progress cards of your week, your weight trend or your photos

BUILD HABITS THAT STICK
• Supplements and habits checklist with reminders and low-stock alerts
• Water tracking with a daily goal
• Logging streaks, a calendar of logged days and weekly insights
• Reminders only when you forget to log a meal or weigh in

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
• No account and no sign-up
• Your food log, weight, measurements and photos stay on your phone
• Back up to a file and restore it on a new phone
• Export your data as CSV, PDF or JSON
• Delete everything in one step whenever you like
• Metric or imperial units, light and dark themes

FITMETRIC PRO
Logging, goals, streaks, widgets, Next-day weight and Goal forecast are free. Every new install includes 30 days of Pro. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and adds:
• Weekly check-in and suggested targets
• What fits?
• Moving average, goal phases and range comparison on weight charts
• Body measurements and progress photos
• Full insights
• Unlimited recipes (free includes 20)
• Backup, restore and export

FitMetric is a tool for tracking food and weight. It isn't medical advice. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant or have a history of disordered eating, talk to a doctor before changing your diet.

Barcode lookups and online food search use Open Food Facts, a free, open database of food products.

Privacy policy: https://dellosaneil.github.io/macrotrack-privacy/
