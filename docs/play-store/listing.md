## Full description (4,000 characters max)

MacroTrack helps you hit your calorie and macro targets every day, without an account, ads or a cluttered screen. Tell it your stats and goal, and it builds your daily calories, protein, carbs and fat for you. Then log what you eat and see exactly what's left.

Everything you log stays on your phone.

LOG FOOD IN SECONDS
• Scan a barcode to find packaged foods instantly
• Search a built-in library of 290 common foods
• Tap a recent food and it remembers the portion you had last time
• Star your favorites for one-tap logging
• Quick add calories or macros when you just know the numbers
• Save your own foods and build recipes from them
• Edit or move an entry with a tap, swipe to delete

SEE WHAT'S LEFT, AT A GLANCE
• Calories and protein, carbs and fat remaining for today
• Meals split into breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks
• Browse any past day and see the goal you had that day

TARGETS BUILT FOR YOU
• Daily calories from your age, height, weight, activity and goal
• Lose, maintain or gain weight, and change your goal any time
• Your goal history is kept, so past days stay accurate

BUILD A HABIT THAT STICKS
• A logging streak on your Today screen
• A calendar showing every logged day and every day on target
• Monthly stats: days logged, days on target and average intake

UNDERSTAND YOUR WEEK
• Calories against your goal for each day of the week
• Average protein, carbs and fat against your targets

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Log your weight and see it by week, month, 3 months or all time
• Set a target weight and follow your progress toward it
• Track body measurements with Pro: waist, chest, hips, arms, thighs and more

YOUR DATA, YOUR PHONE
• No account and no sign-up
• Your food log, weight and measurements never leave your phone
• Back up to a file and restore it on a new phone (Pro)
• Export your data as CSV, PDF or JSON (Pro)
• Delete everything in one step whenever you like
• Metric or imperial units, and light and dark themes

MACROTRACK PRO
Logging, goals, streaks and your full history are free, for good. Every new install includes 30 days of Pro, so you can try everything. Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription, and adds:
• Moving average: the real trend behind daily weight swings
• Goal phases: your weight chart shaded by the goal you had each day
• Range comparison: compare any two days on a weight chart
• Logging body measurements
• Full insights: the monthly view, your meal split and your most-logged foods
• Unlimited recipes (free includes 20)
• Backup, restore and export

MacroTrack is a tool for tracking food and weight. It isn't medical advice. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant or have a history of disordered eating, talk to a doctor before changing your diet.

Barcode lookups use Open Food Facts, a free, open database of food products.
