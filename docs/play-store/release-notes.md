# FitMetric — release notes

Play Console's "What's new" box takes up to 500 characters per language. Each block below fits on its own.

## Next upload (everything since 1.0.0)

For users updating from the first release in one step.

```
MacroTrack is now FitMetric, with a fresh icon.
• Progress photos: albums, a camera guide to line up each shot, a before & after slider and reminders
• Weekly check-in: your real daily burn, a suggested target and your week's calorie balance
• Choose how fast to lose or gain weight
• Log food to past days, plan tomorrow and repeat meals
• Copy a meal or a whole day
• Faster number keypad
• App tour shows example data
```

## 1.0.0 — first release

```
Welcome to MacroTrack!
• Daily calorie and macro targets built from your stats and goal
• Log food by barcode, from a built-in library of 290 foods, or with your own foods and recipes
• Weight history, target weight and body measurements
• Streaks, a logging calendar and weekly insights
• Backup, restore and export
• No account. Your data stays on your phone.
```
